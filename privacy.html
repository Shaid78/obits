Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · OceanBlue IT Studio · Dhaka, Bangladesh

OceanBlue IT Studio ("we", "our", "us") operates the website at oceanblueitstudio.com and provides creative technology services and sourcing & verification services ("OceanBlue Sourcing"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

We collect the information you choose to submit through our contact forms, WhatsApp, WeChat, email or during a project engagement. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp number
  • Your company name and role
  • Project details, supplier information, product descriptions or files you send us
  • Any other information you choose to provide

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, our hosting provider may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit. This is used for security monitoring and to keep the site running reliably.

Information from third parties

If you contact us via WhatsApp, WeChat or email, the platform you use may share limited data with us under their own privacy policies. We do not buy or sell personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • Deliver the services you have requested
  • Communicate about active or proposed projects
  • Issue invoices and process payments
  • Maintain records required for accounting and legal compliance
  • Improve our website and services

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information on the basis of your consent (when you submit an enquiry), the performance of a contract (when you engage us), our legitimate interest in running our business, and our legal obligations under Bangladeshi law.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your information. We share it only when necessary to deliver our services, and only with:

  • Our own team members and, where applicable, subcontractors bound by confidentiality
  • Payment and banking partners (e.g. Payoneer) to process payments
  • Communication platforms you have chosen to use (WhatsApp, WeChat, email)
  • Legal authorities if required by law

5. Sourcing & Verification Engagements

When you engage us for sourcing or verification work, we may need to share limited information with suppliers, factories or local contacts in China in order to complete the engagement. We only share what is strictly necessary — typically your company name, product category and the scope of the visit.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete that information
  • Withdraw consent to marketing or non-essential communications
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at oceanblueits@gmail.com.

8. Cookies and Analytics

Our website does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising analytics. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Transfers

As a Bangladesh-registered business working with international clients and conducting work in China, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected.

11. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · OceanBlue IT Studio · Dhaka, Bangladesh

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations are handled for OceanBlue IT Studio's creative and technology services, and for OceanBlue Sourcing verification & audit services. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Creative & Technology Projects

Before work begins

If you cancel before any work has started, any deposit paid is refunded in full, minus any transaction fees charged by our payment processor.

After work begins

Once work has started, the deposit is non-refundable. Completed milestone payments are also non-refundable, since the work has already been delivered. Any unearned portion of future milestone payments will be refunded.

If we cancel

If we cancel an engagement for any reason, all fees for undelivered work are refunded in full, and any completed work is handed over to you.

2. Sourcing & Verification Services

Sourcing visits require significant advance planning and travel arrangements. The table below explains how cancellations are handled depending on when you notify us.

When you cancelRefund
More than 7 days before the scheduled visitFull refund, minus any transaction fees
3 to 7 days before the scheduled visitRefund of 70%, minus any transaction fees
48 hours to 3 days before the scheduled visitRefund of 50%
Less than 48 hours before the scheduled visitNo refund — non-recoverable travel costs apply
After the visit has taken placeNo refund

Rescheduling instead of cancelling

If you need to reschedule rather than cancel, contact us as soon as possible. In most cases we can move a scheduled visit to a new date without any additional charge, provided we have at least 72 hours' notice and travel has not already been booked.

If we cancel

If we cancel a scheduled visit — for example due to illness, unsafe conditions, or a change in local access — we will offer either a full refund or a free reschedule, at your choice.

3. Extended Video Documentation (+$150)

The optional Extended Video Documentation add-on is prepared after the visit and cannot be cancelled once editing has begun. If you decide not to proceed before editing starts, you will receive a full refund of the add-on fee.

4. Monthly Retainers

  • Retainers are billed monthly at the start of the cycle
  • You may cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice
  • The current month's fee is non-refundable once the cycle has begun
  • Unused hours roll over only as specified in your plan
  • Unused rollover hours are not refundable in cash

5. Non-Refundable Items

The following are never refundable, regardless of circumstance:

  • Third-party travel already booked (rail, flights, hotels)
  • Third-party platform fees, bank charges or payment processor fees
  • Work that has already been delivered and accepted
  • Completed sourcing reports, video documentation, or inspections

6. Quality Concerns

If you are not satisfied with work we have delivered, contact us within 14 days of delivery. We will review the issue and, where reasonable, offer revision, partial credit or a partial refund. Refunds are not automatic — each case is assessed individually.

Our Approach We would rather resolve a problem fairly than lose a client over a dispute. If something hasn't gone to plan, tell us. Most issues can be fixed quickly.

8. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days. The exact time for the funds to reach you depends on your bank or payment provider (Payoneer, Wise, bank transfer, etc.).

9. Currency & Exchange Rate

Refunds are issued in the same currency in which payment was received. We are not responsible for any difference caused by exchange rate movements between payment and refund.

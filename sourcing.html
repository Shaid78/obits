Why Verification Matters

The Problem. The Risk. The Fix.

Step 01 — The Problem

You found a supplier online. Are they real?

Most buyers start on Alibaba, Made-in-China or a trade show lead. The listing looks good. The price is right. But you've never seen the building, met the team, or confirmed the license.

Step 02 — The Risk

Most buyers wire $30,000 before anyone has seen the factory.

A business license can be faked. A factory photo can be borrowed. A sales manager can promise anything. Without on-the-ground verification, you're trusting a screen.

Step 03 — What We Do

We go there. We film it. We report back.

A team member travels to the factory, verifies the license, walks the floor, films the visit with their face visible, and sends you a written report in English — with photos, video link and a clear recommendation.

Step 04 — The Result

You know who you're paying. Before you pay them.

No more guessing. No more trusting a listing. No more wiring money to a company that might not exist. Just a verified report, a video, and a clear path forward.
